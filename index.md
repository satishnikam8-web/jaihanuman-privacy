Privacy Policy for JaiHanuman

Effective Date: August 14, 2026

JaiHanuman ("the App") is a devotional Android application developed to provide users with access to Hanuman-related devotional prayers, readings, audio, video content, and related information.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information associated with your use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

JaiHanuman does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photographs, microphone recordings, or camera data.

The App requests the Android INTERNET permission to communicate with its services and retrieve devotional content and related metadata.

2. Local App Preferences

The App stores certain preferences locally on your device, such as:

Language preference
Theme/display preference
Audio/music preferences
Other application settings

These preferences remain on your device and are not intentionally transmitted to the developer.

You can remove locally stored application data through your Android device settings or by uninstalling the App.

3. Network Services

JaiHanuman may communicate with its application services using standard Android networking technologies to retrieve devotional text and related metadata.

The App does not intentionally transmit personal information to these services.

Network communications may inherently involve technical information required for normal Internet communication, such as IP address and connection information.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

JaiHanuman uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob is a third-party advertising service operated by Google. Depending on Google's applicable policies, settings, and the user's device/configuration, Google may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and advertising-related usage information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

For information about Google's handling of data, please refer to Google's applicable privacy information and advertising controls.

We do not directly receive or store users' personal information through the App for advertising purposes.

5. Audio and Video Content

The devotional videos included in JaiHanuman are bundled within the application and are played locally.

The App does not use YouTube or an external video streaming service to play these bundled devotional videos.

Devotional audio is also played locally or through standard Android media playback services.

The App does not access your microphone or record audio.

6. Data Sharing

JaiHanuman does not sell users' personal information.

The App may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

We do not intentionally share personal information with third parties because the App does not require users to provide such information.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App.

Network communications are performed using standard Android networking mechanisms and secure connections where supported by the service.

Because no online user account is maintained by JaiHanuman, the App does not maintain a developer-controlled database of user profiles.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

JaiHanuman does not maintain user accounts or a developer-controlled personal profile database.

Local application preferences remain on the user's device until they are cleared by the user, the application data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is subject to those services' applicable retention and deletion policies.

9. Children's Privacy

JaiHanuman is a general devotional application and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

10. Third-Party Services

JaiHanuman may use the following third-party service:

Google AdMob — advertising services.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable requirements, or our practices.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be published at this URL and/or made available through the App.

We recommend reviewing this Privacy Policy periodically.
